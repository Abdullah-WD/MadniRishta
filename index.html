About us

الحمدللہ عزوجل – میرا تعلق دعوتِ اسلامی سے ہے

Madni Rishta (Madni Attari Rishtay) ek khaas platform hai jo Sunni Hanfi Barelvi khandanon ko aapas mein jodta hai. Yeh khidmat sachai, imandari aur khuloos-e-niyyat ke saath, sunnat ke mutabiq nikah ko aam karne ke liye ki ja rahi hai. Alhamdulillah, guzishta 8 saalon se nikah karwane mein masroof-e-amal hain.

Hamari koshish

Nikah aam ho aur zina khatam ho. Har behen beti ka ghar abaad ho, lekin yeh aap logon ke taawun se hi mumkin hai. Yani demands kam karein aur nikah ko sunnat ke mutabiq ada karein.

Hamari pehchaan

Dawat-e-Islami · Attari · Sunni Hanfi Barelvi · Sahih-ul-Aqeedah.

Hamara usool

Main bad-mazhab ya bad-aqeedah logon ke naam aur tareeqe ko ikhtiyar nahi kar sakta, kyunke mujhe Dawat-e-Islami, Attariyon aur tamam sahih-ul-aqeedah Sunni Hanfi Barelvi Musalmanon se dili mohabbat hai. Hum Sahaba-e-Kiram, Ahl-e-Bait-e-Athar, Auliya-e-Kiram aur tamam Ambiya-e-Kiram (عليهم السلام) se mohabbat rakhne wale hain. Isi liye meri koshish hai ke sahih-ul-aqeedah, nek, deendar aur sunnaton par amal karne wale logon ke darmiyan nikah karwaye jayein, taake gharon mein mohabbat, sukoon aur barkat aaye.

Zaati channel ki wazahat

Yeh Muhammad Shahid Attari (Arab Sharif) ki zaati koshish aur zaati channel hai. Madani Channel ka koi official rishta ya zimmedari is se mansoob nahi.

Dua: Allah Pak mera imaan salamat rakhe, mujhe hamesha maslak-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat par qaim rakhe, aur Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat Damat Barakatuhumul Aaliyah ko salamat aur afiyat wali umr-e-daraz ata farmaye. Ameen bijah-e-Khatamun Nabiyyeen ﷺ.
