          About us

          Alhamdulillah – I belong to Dawat-e-Islami

          Our Mission

          May nikah become common and zina disappear. May the home of every sister and daughter be settled, but this is only possible with your cooperation – that is, reduce demands and perform nikah according to the Sunnah.

          I cannot adopt the name or way of people of corrupt belief, because I have deep love for Dawat-e-Islami, the Attariyyin and all Muslims of sound belief of the Sunni Hanfi Barelvi school. We love the Sahaba-e-Kiram, the Ahl-e-Bait-e-Athar, the Auliya-e-Kiram and all the Ambiya-e-Kiram (عليهم السلام). That is why my effort is to arrange nikah among people of sound belief who are pious, religious and follow the Sunnah, so that love, peace and blessings come to their homes.
